01 — THE LETTER

Dear Kanishka,

I hope you never forget that there is a whole person inside you who deserves your attention, patience and love.

I know you have spent time trying to understand other people, their emotions, their decisions and their expectations. But have you spent enough time understanding yourself?

I want you to discover who you are when nobody is watching, when nobody is appreciating you, when nobody is choosing you.

I want you to build a life that excites you. A life filled with knowledge, ambition, friendships, experiences, freedom and meaningful work.

You can love people deeply. You can welcome companionship. But never make another person's presence the condition for your own happiness.

This time, I want you to choose yourself. Not out of anger, not out of disappointment, but out of genuine respect for your own existence.

With love, from you. ♡
02 — THE LITTLE GIRL

To the little girl within me.

There was a little girl who wanted affection, attention, tenderness and the feeling of being special. I don't want to dismiss her needs anymore.

I want to understand her, listen to her and give her the patience she deserves.

✧

She wanted attention.

She wanted to be noticed without having to compete for it.

Today, I will learn to notice my own needs and express them.

♡

She wanted tenderness.

She wanted to be pampered, cared for and understood.

Today, I can offer myself tenderness without feeling guilty.

☾

She wanted to be heard.

She wanted her emotions to be acknowledged.

Today, I don't have to dismiss my feelings just because someone else cannot understand them.

✦

She wanted to feel important.

She wanted to know that she mattered.

Today, my importance is not something I need to earn.

I am not ashamed of the little girl I was.

03 — THE WOMAN I AM BECOMING

I have a life to build.

I am more than my relationships. I am a mind that wants to learn, a person with ambitions, values, curiosity and an entire future to explore.

⚖

My intellectual world

Knowledge, law, research, questions and independent thinking.

I will never stop learning and questioning.

✦

My ambitions

My professional dreams, challenges and achievements.

I have goals that belong entirely to me.

∞

My freedom

Making decisions, exploring the world and experiencing life.

I am allowed to choose the shape of my own life.

☼

My inner strength

Self-respect, courage, emotional maturity and resilience.

I can be gentle without surrendering my boundaries.

04 — WHEN YOU NEED A REMINDER

Read this slowly.

You do not have to abandon yourself to be loved.

A small reminder, whenever you need one.

05 — MY NON-NEGOTIABLES

Things I never want to forget.

I will not make someone else's opinion the final authority over my decisions.

I will not confuse attention with love, or dependence with connection.

I will allow myself to receive love without surrendering my identity.

I will take responsibility for my choices and learn from my mistakes.

I will build a life that I find meaningful, even when nobody is watching.

✧ GROW · ✦ LEARN · ☾ LIVE · ✧
06 — A PROMISE

One day, I want to look at my life and know that I never gave up on myself.

I don't have to have everything figured out today. I just have to keep showing up for the person I am becoming.

I am my own home. ♡

Dear Kanishka, keep growing.
